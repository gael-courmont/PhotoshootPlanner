# Glossary

**Client**:
A prospective customer, usually a tourist on a phone, who builds a photowalk before booking.
_Avoid_: user, visitor

**Photographer**:
The business owner who defines the packages and spots and takes the photos.
_Avoid_: admin, owner

**Photowalk**:
A guided photo session on foot through a chosen set of spots.
_Avoid_: shoot, tour

**Package**:
A photowalk defined only by its duration, which is the client's time budget. Carries no default spots or other rules.
_Avoid_: plan, offer, formula

**Spot**:
A named shooting location with a description, sample photos and a fixed shooting time.
_Avoid_: location, place, stop

**Shooting time**:
The minutes the photographer reserves at a spot. Fixed per spot; the client cannot change it.
_Avoid_: duration, dwell time

**Start spot**:
The spot the client picks to begin the photowalk. Always first in the route.
_Avoid_: origin, meeting point

**Selection**:
The client's current choice of package, start spot and other spots. Shareable by link.
_Avoid_: cart, itinerary

**Leg**:
The walk between two spots, with its walking time and distance.
_Avoid_: segment, step

**Route**:
The ordered walk from the start spot through the selected spots, made of legs.
_Avoid_: path, itinerary

**Time budget**:
The package duration measured against shooting time plus walking time of the selection. Under 90% is green, 90–100% amber, over 100% red.
_Avoid_: quota

**Hand-off**:
Passing the client's selection to the existing booking app, via a copyable summary. Booking itself is out of scope.
_Avoid_: checkout, booking

**Walking time**:
The minutes needed to walk a leg: the routing service's estimate rounded up to whole minutes, unless the photographer has overridden it.
_Avoid_: travel time, transit time

**Override**:
The photographer's manual correction of a leg's walking time, for real-world quirks such as stairs, crowds or a closed bridge.
_Avoid_: patch, adjustment
